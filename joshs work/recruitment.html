Join Our Team – Protect Forests, Empower Communities

At A New Green World, our mission is rooted in forest conservation and ecological restoration. We're looking for passionate individuals who want to make a real impact on climate action, biodiversity, and community resilience through nature-based solutions.

Forest Conservation Coordinator

Location: Central Forest Reserves (Field-based)

Type: Full-time

Salary: Shs.600,000 - Shs.800,000 per year

Description: Coordinate conservation efforts across key forest areas including monitoring, awareness campaigns, and reforestation planning. Collaborate with local communities and environmental authorities to ensure sustainable forest use and restoration.

Responsibilities:

  • Plan and implement reforestation projects
  • Engage local communities in forest protection
  • Conduct field surveys and biodiversity assessments
  • Prepare progress reports and conservation proposals

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Forestry, Environmental Science, or related field
  • Strong organizational and communication skills
  • Experience with GIS or remote sensing is an added advantage
  • Willingness to work in remote, forested environments

Environmental Educator

Location: Various locations (Remote options available)

Type: Full-time

Salary: shs.405,000 - shs.550,000 per year

Description: Develop and deliver forest conservation education programs for schools and communities. Promote sustainable practices and inspire the next generation of forest stewards.

Responsibilities:

  • Create lesson plans and workshops focused on forest ecosystems
  • Work with schools and communities to run campaigns and training
  • Develop printed and digital educational materials
  • Monitor and assess program impact

Requirements:

  • Degree in Environmental Education, Forestry, or related field
  • Experience in public speaking or teaching
  • Strong communication and leadership skills
  • Passion for forest and climate action

Volunteer Opportunities

Be part of our forest conservation mission through volunteer work. Roles include:

  • Tree planting in degraded areas
  • Forest clean-up campaigns
  • Awareness and advocacy in local schools
  • Documenting wildlife and plant biodiversity

No experience required — just commitment to a greener planet!
