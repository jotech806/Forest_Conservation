Knowledge is power when it comes to forest conservation. Below you'll find comprehensive information about key forest issues and what you can do to help.

Why Forests Matter

Forests are vital to life on Earth. They clean our air, filter our water, prevent erosion, and provide a habitat for wildlife. Forest conservation is essential for maintaining biodiversity and combating climate change.

What You Can Do

  • Plant trees in your community.
  • Support sustainable wood products.
  • Reduce, reuse, and recycle paper.
  • Participate in or donate to forest conservation efforts.
